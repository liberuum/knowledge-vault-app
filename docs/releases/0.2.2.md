### What's new

- **Updates come to you.** When a new release is out, a notice drops from the top of the window. **Update**
  downloads the installer for your computer, with its progress, and installs it; **Later** keeps your session.
  The app checks when it starts and every 90 minutes while it is open. This works from 0.2.2 on.
- **API keys for every service.** OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini and xAI keys now list their models as soon as
  you type the key, inside their card. Each model shows whether it can process sources (*fits processing*,
  *won't work*, with the reason, or *unchecked*), and the best one at a fair price is chosen for you. Anthropic
  and Gemini keys work fully, OpenAI's newest models process sources, and every model is told to answer in the
  format processing needs.
- **Clear errors.** A vault that can't open says why in plain words and offers the way back — *Try again*,
  *Sign in again* or *Back to vaults* — instead of a raw error. A brief hiccup at Renown is retried by itself.
- **The chat keeps going.** Switch to another view while the chat answers, come back, and the answer is still
  arriving, or finished, with its sources.
- **A new loading screen** while a vault, a note or Workflow Studio opens: a small vault graph coming together.
- **The setup guide's example** is ready on the list of sources from the start, so you can try the app without
  a file of your own.
- **Linux:** smooth scrolling on machines with a graphics card again; the slower renderer is used only where the
  graphics card's one can't run.
