### What's new

- **Models on a computer of yours, including over a VPN.** A model server on another of your computers now works over Tailscale and similar VPNs, by its address (`100.x.y.z`) or its name (MagicDNS, like `gpu-box.tailnet.ts.net`), and on your local network by name (like `nas.local`). It is treated like a model on this computer: no key is sent to it, and it works on one request and one source at a time.
