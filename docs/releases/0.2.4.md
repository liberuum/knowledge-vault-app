### What's new

- **A new vault layout.** Everything now lives in one sidebar, with no bar across the top: Ask, Notes, Topics,
  Sources, Work, Graph and Contradictions, your recent chats, and Vault settings at the foot. Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac)
  searches notes, topics, sources and chats from anywhere, and can ask the vault straight from the search box.
- **Topics and Contradictions pages.** Topics lists every topic largest first, with the overview on top.
  Contradictions shows each pair of conflicting notes side by side, with why they disagree, and opens them to
  resolve or dissolve.
- **An easier-to-read chat.** The conversation sits on its own card, answers are larger, and what each answer used
  is listed under it.
- **Sources open at once.** The list, its folder counts and the timeline come from one request instead of reading
  every source first: a vault with over a thousand sources shows its list in a third of a second.
- **A timeline of what you added.** Sources › Timeline shows each file, paste or feed you added, when, and the notes
  and topics it became.
- **Long books convert much faster.** A 1,000-page PDF now converts in under 6 minutes instead of 15: big PDFs are
  read in page windows with a progress bar, and the index and glossary are no longer read as tables. Letters the
  reader used to drop ("ﬁeld" read as "eld") are put back.
- **Adding many sources is faster,** about twice as fast for a large file, and an add that a restart or reload cuts
  off carries on by itself. Quitting while sources are being added asks first.
- **Recordings become sources.** Audio files (MP3, M4A, WAV, …) and the soundtrack of videos are transcribed on this
  computer and added as Transcript sources.
- **Starter workflows.** The setup guide can add a feed, papers or meeting notes to a vault.
- **Clearer processing status.** The status chip says what the work is doing now, and Update brings a vault's
  pipeline up to date without losing its history.
- **Scrolling on Linux** keeps pace with the mouse wheel.
- **Built on Powerhouse 6.2.3-dev.60** and docling.rs 2.30.1.
